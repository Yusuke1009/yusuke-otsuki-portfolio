## Phase 02

- Label: Akari's Record
- Period: 2025.02 — Ongoing
- Headline: Documenting my child's growth with a weekly edited video
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJMqvjslGXI&list=PLLTZwZrn9tj1GDHADgN7zCOTRXIBpEX_f&index=35

Since the day my daughter was born, I have edited each week's moments into a video, every single week. Before I knew it, I had passed 60 videos. I simply love making and sharing things as part of everyday life.

### Achievements

- Ongoing at a pace of one video per week (over 60 videos)
- Turned shooting → selecting → editing → exporting into a routine
