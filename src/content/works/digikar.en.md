## Overview

From 2,000 to 10,000 facilities. Driving the growth of the No. 1 market-share cloud EHR
on a team that had gone five years without a designer

## Phase 01

- Label: Phase 1.1 — Embedding the value of a designer
- Period: 2020.10 — 2020.12
- Headline: On a team without a designer for five years, start by building trust

Earning trust one step at a time. I held 1-on-1s with every team member and ran workshops to surface problems a designer could solve right away.
Starting with CS support improvements, release notes, and building an operational structure, I let the team experience firsthand that "having a designer makes the team move."

And because changing an EHR's design carries a high risk of disrupting the habits users have built up,
I first took on more approachable work — developing and improving companion apps — and stacked up small wins.

### Achievements

- Surfaced UX improvement opportunities in the core M3 DigiKar product and designed new features
- Built the operational workflow for release notes and support pages
- Released the iPhone app "M3 DigiKar Mobile" within one month of joining (★4.3)
- Relaunched the iPad app "M3 DigiKar Handwritten Chart" (★4.4)

## Phase 02

- Label: Phase 1.2 — Embedding user understanding across the organization
- Period: 2021
- Headline: Building a culture of going to the field. Toward a team that can speak about firsthand information in its own words

The team had no habit of going to the field. I designed surveys myself and interviewed 20 physicians. After building those relationships, I opened up routes for on-site observation and began visiting the field frequently. Once I could speak about firsthand information in my own words, I took the step of doubling as Product Manager.

### Achievements

- 20+ physician interviews, plus new routes opened for on-site observation
- Embedded a user research culture in the team
- Started a dual role as PdM (participating in product strategy)

### Images

- /assets/works/digikar/02-observation.webp | Valuing observation and turning it into culture. The product grows by finding and delivering what users truly want.

## Phase 03

- Label: Phase 2 — Leading the business as PdM while leading design
- Period: 2022 — 2024
- Headline: Leading business growth as a team of four. Charting the Next Carve

Led business growth in a four-person structure: lead PdM, design PdM, engineering PdM, and CS lead. Working agilely on monthly cycles — from release prioritization, requirements definition, and engineer assignment to design prototypes and release articles — we carried the strategy and planning for the Next Carve all the way through to execution.

### Achievements

- Rebranding; built out the design system and guidelines
- Marketing planning and execution (PDCA on the Black Jack whitepaper campaign)
- Front-end overhaul of the core DigiKar product (Angular → React)
- Designed, coordinated across departments, and led RME (real medical education), the core revenue driver
