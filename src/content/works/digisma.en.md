## Phase 02

- Label: Phase 2 — From PMF to scale
- Period: 2022 — 2024
- Headline: Going solo, leading to PMF

When the dedicated designer left after launch, I became a one-person design team.
I led design for core feature development and organized branding assets together with a BX designer.
I also updated the site to build out the brand's world, and won the Good Design Award, pursued for its marketing impact.

Within a fast-paced agile structure shipping weekly releases, I drove roughly 5–10 new features and improvements per month.
I also renewed the design from its MVP state, improving key KPIs (active usage, bookings, payments) while making the style clearer and easier for anyone to understand.

On top of that, I personally interviewed and hired the designer who would carry Digisma forward.
Through daily 1-on-1s and onboarding support, I fully handed over the role of product lead designer.
Today, all of Digisma's design can be entrusted to them.

### Achievements

- Design lead through to PMF
- Rebranding (jointly with BX / site update)
- Won the Good Design Award
- Design renewal (published as a Cocoda article)
- Designer hiring → onboarding → building a structure for full handoff
