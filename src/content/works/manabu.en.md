## Overview

"manabu" is a medical platform co-developed with VIDAL, a group company based in France.
After running validation experiments in each country, we launched a medical media product around "learning" — a theme of high interest to physicians around the world.

## Phase 01

- Label: Phase 1 — Launching the MVP
- Period: 2021
- Headline: Bringing the world's medical information into one place. 0 → 1 as a global product

To shape the world the product aims for, we started by defining design principles.
I led the work end to end — tuning information density and typography while validating readability for physicians, through layout and component design built for future extensibility.
Handling front-end implementation as well as design, I got the MVP launched in the shortest possible time.

### Achievements

- Established design principles (building on the M3 taste, toward a more refined, universal design)
- Research on information density and fonts, and exploration of design variations
- Layout and component design with extensibility in mind
- Front-end implementation in React
