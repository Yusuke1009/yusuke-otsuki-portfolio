## Overview

Six years spent taking the "broad-to-narrow range of design skills" learned in Design Science at Chiba University Graduate School and bringing them into hands-on practice at JVCKENWOOD Design and PickNote. The original form of a designer hungry for design's possibilities.

## Phase 01

- Label: Chiba University Graduate School, Design Science
- Period: 2014 — 2018
- Headline: The origin of my design practice — grasping design from its broadest to its narrowest sense

Started in mechanical engineering, then turned to design after meeting a mentor. Through studying abroad at Köln International School of Design (integrated design) and Politecnico di Milano (design engineering), practiced broadly across design research, UI, graphics, and industrial design. Through competitions and industry-academia collaboration — from future forecasting to concrete industrial products — I developed a design foundation that moves back and forth between the abstract and the concrete.

### Achievements

- soete — an air purifier that lets you truly feel "the air is clean" (prizewinner, Next-Generation Air Purifier Design Competition)
- drip3 — a personal coffee maker for the desk (Politecnico di Milano, design engineering)
- Field study & solution proposal for Singapore's integrated resorts (with Panasonic)
- Vision design for cities, transportation, and stations in 2030 (with HITACHI)

## Phase 02

- Label: JVCKENWOOD Design
- Period: 2018.4 — 2019.12
- Headline: Creating work from self-initiated research within a design organization of nearly 100

Handled vision concepting / concept development / interaction design / video production for a wide range of stakeholders, from business units and the IP department to affiliated companies and a major automaker. Led R&D on TouchDesigner and drove its official in-house adoption starting from self-initiated activity (4 pro licenses). This was the period in which I established my pattern of "creating work from self-initiated research."

### Achievements

- Wireless solutions projection-mapping exhibit — experience design combining a 3D city model with sensor interactivity, self-developed in TouchDesigner
- Video introducing the history of the wireless radio business — history laid out across the space, structured so the video could expand into an exhibition presentation tool
- Forest Flow — kansei (affective) design research, TouchDesigner / Unity prototypes
- Maker Faire 2019 "Forest Notes," a prototype for feeling the forest — led the team and conducted fieldwork in Yamanashi
- "Extend Your Ears with Technology," a workshop at Daikanyama Teens Creative — an ear-worn device for ASMR experiences
- Vision concepting workshop — generated 74 scene ideas

## Phase 03

- Label: PickNote, Inc.
- Period: 2020.1 — 2020.6
- Headline: Launching a service from zero at a 4-person startup

As designer and front-end engineer, I took a new product combining AI cameras with digital signage from planning through implementation. Taught myself Vue / Vuetify / Firebase / Node.js in 2 months and built an SPA. When the pandemic made it difficult to sustain the business, I left and joined M3.

### Achievements

- "pick," an AI camera × digital signage & in-store analytics service — built single-handedly, from UX/UI through the SPA implementation and Firebase backend
- "Catto," an interactive bulletin board for shared offices — touchless operation via an edge AI camera, implemented in Qt / Qml
- Won a project from the shared office MOV, taking it from prototype → PDCA → delivery
- Learned programming for embedded devices from scratch and shipped implementations
